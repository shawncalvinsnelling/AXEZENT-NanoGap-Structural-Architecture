# Model and image provenance

**Author displayed publicly:** Shawn Calvin Snelling. **Design family:** AXEZENT Quantum Cube v1.0. **Historical source-model date:** July 23–24, 2026. **Research website release preparation:** November 11, 2026.

## Photographed sample

- `assets/images/printed-cube-original.png` is the unmodified photo submitted by the author in an email follow-up to Professor Carlos M. Portela and Professor Albert-László Barabási on November 11, 2026 with the message *“Model I already 3d printed.”*
- `assets/images/printed-cube.webp` is an optimized web-display copy of the same photograph (no simulated content added).
- The photograph depicts a small white porous cube on a tabletop. Photograph alone does not establish exact specimen geometry, production method, material properties, dimensions, or mechanical performance.

## Render

- `assets/images/cad-render.webp` is a web-optimized copy of the separately supplied real AXEZENT STL render.
- `assets/images/schematic.svg` is an independently drawn explanatory diagram; **not** a photograph, structural simulation or exact STL cross section.

## Archived filenames

The original machine-readable source and STL model files previously included `AXZ_Dr_Shawn_...` in filenames or source defaults. The current site attributes all project authorship to **Shawn Calvin Snelling**; archived names are not silently changed in the source/manifest because that would affect reproducibility. Public download names of two binary STL copies are updated for presentation only; underlying mesh contents are unchanged.
